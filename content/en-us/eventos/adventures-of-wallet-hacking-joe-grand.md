This talk is part of LICS ENIGMA.

We are honored to welcome Joe Grand (also known as Kingpin), a legendary hardware hacker, computer engineer, and former member of the iconic hacker collective L0pht Heavy Industries.

With decades of experience on the front lines of cybersecurity, from his historic 1998 testimony before the US Senate to running Grand Idea Studio, Joe specializes in reverse engineering hardware implants, exposing vulnerabilities, and executing complex fault injection attacks.

In his brand-new talk, "Adventures of Wallet Hacking: The Final Chapter," he will take us on a thrilling journey into his most famous cryptocurrency recoveries.

Joe will share deep insights into how fault injection and side-channel attacks were utilized in the most recent Trezor One and Model T hacks, alongside other fascinating wallet recovery stories.
