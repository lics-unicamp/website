This talk is part of LICS ENIGMA.

We are honored to welcome Uirá Ribeiro, Chair of the Board of Directors at the Linux Professional Institute (LPI), CEO at Certificação Linux, and a renowned DevOps and Linux Expert.

With over 27 years of experience in IT, cloud computing architecture, and information security, Uirá holds a Master's in Computer Science and 15 major industry certifications (including RHCE, LPIC-3, AWS, and CySA+).

He is also a professor and the author of 11 books recognized in the open-source software industry.

In his exclusive session, "Your first step in cybersecurity: LPI Security Essentials," he will guide us through the fundamental concepts of security and how to build a solid foundation to protect systems and kickstart your career in cybersecurity.
