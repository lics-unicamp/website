Currently working as a Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) Analyst at Apura Cyber Intelligence, Mariana specializes in cyber threat analysis, open-source intelligence (OSINT) investigations, and intelligence production to anticipate risks and support strategic decision-making.

She brings a strong commitment to the cybersecurity community and knowledge sharing, serving as a Cyber Ninja at WOMCY, LATAM Women in Cybersecurity, and contributing technical articles to renowned publications such as Hakin9 Magazine and PenTest Magazine.

In her talk, “Beyond the Attack: Geopolitics and Its Impact on Cyber Threat Intelligence,” she will share her strategic and practical perspective on how to turn data into action, exploring the use of intelligence to support decision-making in an increasingly complex digital landscape.
