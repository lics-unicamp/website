**This talk will be held in Portuguese**

This talk is part of LICS ENIGMA.

We are honored to welcome Jullyano Lino, an IBM Qiskit Advocate, Cybersecurity Consultant at the Inter-American Development Bank (IADB), and a specialist in quantum technologies.

With extensive experience on the front lines of information security across major government institutions in Brazil, including the Presidency of the Republic, INSS, and Enap, Jullyano holds an MSc in Quantum Computing and carries top-tier certifications like CISSP and C|TIA.

His groundbreaking research explores the intersection of quantum sensing applied to national defense and quantum protocols.

In his exclusive session, "Mini-course - First Steps: Quantum Cybersecurity," he will take us on a thrilling journey into the foundations of quantum computing, the evolution of quantum threats, and how to defend against them.
