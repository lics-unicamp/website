**This talk will be held in Portuguese**

This talk is part of LICS ENIGMA.

We are honored to welcome Fernando Silva, Senior Windows Kernel Engineer, Founder of DriverEntry Kernel Development, and an expert with over 20 years of experience in low-level C/C++ software.

With key projects at Venn, HCLSoftware, and IBM, Fernando is an authority in Windows Internals, driver development (WDM/KMDF), and advanced debugging with WinDbg and IDA Pro.

In his session, "Uma Espiada no Kernel do Windows" (A Peek into the Windows Kernel), he will break down OS architecture, driver mechanics, and how security operates directly at Ring 0.
