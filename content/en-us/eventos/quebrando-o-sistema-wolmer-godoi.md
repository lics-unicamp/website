We are honored to welcome Wolmer Godoi, a cybersecurity executive (CISSP) with more than 28 years of experience leading MSSP, SOC, GRC, and Red Team portfolios across Latin America and Europe.

Currently serving as Associate Partner and Data & Application Security Competency Leader at IBM, Wolmer specializes in Zero Trust, data protection, and cloud security, with a strong focus on Quantum-Safe strategies and risk mitigation.

He brings extensive strategic and technical experience, having served as a CTO and led the development of global CSIRT and threat intelligence operations in highly complex corporate environments.

In addition to his strong frontline corporate presence, including notable participation in Security Leaders, he has deep ties to the hacker and InfoSec communities. He is a frequent speaker and the driving force behind discussions at major conferences such as BSides São Paulo (Quantum Village BR) and Hacking na Web Day.

In his talk, “Breaking the System: The Era of Quantum Hacking,” he will provide a critical analysis of the evolution of quantum computing and its real-world impact on digital security, demystifying the quantum apocalypse while highlighting the practical challenges of post-quantum architectures.
