**This talk will be held in Portuguese**

This talk is part of LICS ENIGMA.

We are honored to welcome Yuri Diógenes, an industry titan with over 30 years of experience in IT and cybersecurity leadership. With a PhD in Cybersecurity Leadership, Yuri is a University Professor at The University of Texas at Dallas and EC-Council University, shaping the next generation of professionals.

Having recently concluded a remarkable 20+ year journey at Microsoft, where he was a Principal PM Manager leading global teams for products like Defender for Cloud, Yuri is a true global authority in Cloud Security, CNAPP, and AI Security.

As if that wasn't enough, he is the author of 36 cybersecurity books, including his latest releases, "Leading AI Transformation" and "The Cybersecurity Executive's First Year Playbook".

In his session, "AI Security Considerations", he will break down the challenges of Agentic AI, risk management, identity-first security, and how organizations can establish robust governance without slowing down technological innovation.
