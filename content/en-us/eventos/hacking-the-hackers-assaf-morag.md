We are honored to welcome Assaf Morag, a renowned security researcher and Threat Intelligence Consultant, with an outstanding track record of hunting advanced malware, botnets, and complex cybercrime operations.

With extensive experience on the front lines of cybersecurity, including his recent role as Director of Security Research at Aqua Security and now Security Researcher at Flare, Assaf specializes in tracking and exposing the tactics and infrastructures used by today's most sophisticated cybercriminals.

He is also a contributor to the MITRE ATT&CK® Container Framework.

In his talk, "Hacking Hackers Who Hack Hackers: Supply-Chain Backdoors in Underground VPN Infrastructure," he will replicate the exact same presentation delivered at this year's DEF CON 34.

Assaf will share deep insights into a fascinating scenario: attackers hacking attackers.

He will reveal the behind-the-scenes of how supply-chain backdoors are ingeniously deployed inside underground VPN infrastructures, compromising the hackers themselves.
