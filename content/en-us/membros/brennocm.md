Hello agent. Thanks for making it this far - Tolstoy needed over a thousand pages for *War and Peace*; I will try to be brief.

## Who I Am
I am a Security Engineer and information security researcher. What drives me is finding the spot where a system trusts something too much - a forgotten IAM permission, an oracle nobody monitors, a signature made blindly - and building tools that surface that spot before an attacker does.

Since 2025 I have been working at Serasa Experian on identity and access: I automate IAM assurance workflows, implement role-based access control and maintain identity governance (SailPoint) and privileged access management (CyberArk) platforms. Before that, I was at Darede's SOC (2023 - 2024), working on vulnerability management, SIEM operations, incident response playbooks and AWS security services.

I study Systems Analysis and Development at FT/UNICAMP, which I reached through ProFIS. Along the way I collected a few certifications: Security+, eJPT, MCRTA, CBP, CCSP-AWS, CNSP, CAP and AWS Cloud Practitioner.

## What I Build
I write small tools, mostly in Go or Rust, for problems I ran into myself:

- [escalint](https://github.com/brennocm/escalint) - maps how a principal could become admin in an AWS account from a read-only IAM snapshot.
- [blindsight](https://github.com/brennocm/blindsight) - verifies Safe multisig transactions offline, before you sign.
- [oraclint](https://github.com/brennocm/oraclint) and [oraclewatch](https://github.com/brennocm/oraclewatch) - static linter and monitoring for Chainlink node deployments and Data Feeds.
- [PwnCortex](https://github.com/brennocm/pwncortex) - centralizes an entire pentest, with a local LLM that answers questions about the target.

The rest (16 projects in total), plus articles and write-ups, lives on [my website](https://brennocm.github.io/).
